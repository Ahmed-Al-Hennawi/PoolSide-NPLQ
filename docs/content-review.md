## Important distinctions

- RLSS implemented updated CPR and first aid content from 1 May 2026. The screenshots are predominantly from May 2026 course slides.
- Adult unresponsiveness prompts an immediate 999 call, followed by breathing assessment while the call connects; do not wait to confirm abnormal breathing before calling.
- Paediatric compression-to-ventilation ratios depend on specific training. The text distinguishes PBLS-trained 15:2 from otherwise 30:2 and directs learners to their current approved sequence.
- Infant chest compressions use the current two-thumb encircling approach.
- Drowning and paediatric resuscitation have initial rescue-breath considerations distinct from the ordinary adult cardiac-arrest sequence.
- Screenshots IMG_2053 and IMG_2057 give broad recovery-position advice in trauma contexts. The revision text follows current RCUK guidance: do not routinely recovery-position trauma; airway needs and minimising spinal movement take priority.
- Natural Scan:20 and rotation figures are training guidance used within local procedures, not universal statutory limits.

## Limits

Automated extraction can misread small slide text. Lessons consolidate related screenshots rather than transcribing each one. Physical rescue techniques require trainer-led practice. No independent clinical review, legal certification, formal WCAG audit or official RLSS endorsement is claimed. Recheck official sources and course materials when updating this content.
